# Time-series analysis

Time series may contain trend, seasonality, autocorrelation, holidays, and structural breaks. Compare like-for-like periods, aggregate at a meaningful interval, and keep chronological ordering intact. A single before/after comparison can confuse seasonality with a sustained change.

State the covered time range and aggregation. Forecast validation should train only on past observations and test on later periods. Concurrent changes can coincide with a decline without causing it.
