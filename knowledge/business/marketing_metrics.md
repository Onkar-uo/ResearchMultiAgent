# Marketing metrics

Spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, and acquired customers describe different stages of a marketing funnel. Spend is an input, not a direct measure of effective reach or incremental sales. Outcomes may lag campaigns, and channel mix, attribution windows, promotions, pricing, and seasonality can confound simple comparisons.

Use holdout experiments or credible quasi-experimental designs to estimate incremental impact. Correlation between spend and sales can be positive, negative, or absent because budgets may respond to expected demand. Predictive feature importance does not estimate marketing return on investment.
