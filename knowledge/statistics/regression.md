# Regression interpretation

Regression models describe conditional associations or make predictions. Coefficients depend on model form, coding, included covariates, measurement error, and data selection. A predictive fit does not establish that changing a predictor would change the outcome.

Inspect residuals, nonlinearity, heteroskedasticity, influential observations, multicollinearity, leakage, and out-of-sample performance. Include relevant confounders only when justified and available; statistical adjustment cannot guarantee removal of unmeasured confounding. Communicate estimates with uncertainty and the population to which they apply.
