# ANOVA and group comparisons

One-way ANOVA tests whether at least one group mean differs under independence, approximate residual normality, and variance assumptions. A significant omnibus result does not identify which groups differ; planned contrasts or multiplicity-adjusted post-hoc comparisons are needed.

Report group sizes, means, variability, effect size, and uncertainty. For strongly non-normal or ordinal outcomes, consider robust or rank-based methods. Unequal group sizes and confounding can distort simple comparisons. Group mean differences are associations unless the study design supports causal interpretation.
